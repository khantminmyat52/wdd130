Myanmar

Myanmar, also historically known as Burma, is a culturally rich and geographically diverse nation located in Southeast Asia, bordering the Andaman Sea and the Bay of Bengal. It is characterized by its stunning landscape of fertile plains, rugged mountains and the vital Irrawaddy River, alongside thousands of shimmering Buddhist pagodas most notably the Shwedagon Pagoda in its former capital, Yangon. Home to over a hundred distinct ethnic groups, the country possesses a deeply rooted heritage reflected in its traditional arts, cuisine and customs. While Myanmar boasts immense natural beauty and vast resources like jade, teak and gemstones, its modern history has been heavily shaped by complex political transitions and ongoing internal conflicts.
