Our Exciting White Water Rafting Trips

Salmon River Family Splash

Perfect for families and beginners, this gentle trip offers scenic beauty and mild rapids (Class I-II). Enjoy swimming in calm river pools, spotting local wildlife and a delicious riverside picnic. It is a safe, fun and relaxing experience designed to create lifelong memories for all ages.

Canyon Rush Expedition

Get ready for an exciting full-day adventure through stunning rock canyons and thrilling Class III rapids. Guided by our expert river professionals, you will navigate famous drops and rolling waves. This trip balances heart-pounding action with beautiful wilderness scenery.

Thunder Gorge Extreme Weekend

Designed only for experienced rafters seeking the ultimate adrenaline rush! This multi-day journey challenges you with intense, technical Class IV-V rapids. Tackle powerful waterfalls, steep drops, and fast-moving currents, then camp under the stars for the ultimate outdoor thrill.
